CN-05: Whispers on the Wire

NetworkingModerate-Hard

The host found in CN-04 sits in CeylonGov Secure's internal service segment. Investigators have a packet capture from that segment. Recover the intruder's credentials from it, then use them against the live service on the staging host.

How to start

Goal: Recover the intruder login from the capture, then use it on the live FTP service.

  1. Download evidence_capture.pcap and open it in Wireshark.
  2. Filter on ftp and follow the TCP stream to find the username and password.
  3. The FTP service runs on the same machine as this site, on port 2221. Connect with an FTP client, or in PowerShell run: curl.exe ftp://USERNAME:PASSWORD@localhost:2221/
  4. Download flag.txt from the server. It holds the flag and points you to CN-06.
