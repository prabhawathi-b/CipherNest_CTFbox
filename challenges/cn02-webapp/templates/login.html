How to start

Goal: get into this legacy portal without a valid login. You are not given a username or password.

  1. Study the login form and see how it reacts to unusual input.
  2. The form passes what you type straight into a database query. Look up SQL injection (OWASP) and try the simplest authentication bypass.
  3. Once you are in, read the maintenance notes. They hold the flag and the clue for CN-03.

Hints

Hint 1

The login form trusts what you type more than it should.

Hint 2

Try breaking out of the SQL query with a single quote.

Hint 3

A classic OR condition in the username field will get you further than a password guess.
